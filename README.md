# vidquest
VidQuest is a Gen-Z-focused super-app that transforms passive short-form video scrolling into an interactive, gamified earning experience. Built to bridge the gap between content creators and highly engaged audiences, the platform redefines the creator economy by actively rewarding users for their attention and comprehension.The application centers around a dynamic Teaser Feed, where users scroll through engaging 15-second video previews. This feed acts as the primary discovery engine. If a user watches a teaser to completion, an interactive "Hunt Quiz" button activates, seamlessly routing them to the creator’s full-length YouTube video to hunt for specific clues. By correctly answering a multiple-choice trivia quiz based directly on the video’s content—requiring a passing score of at least 4 out of 5—users earn digital coins securely credited to their platform wallet.                                                                              Users can redeem their earned balance through a tiered withdrawal system designed for practical, real-world value:

Tier 1 (₹40): Direct mobile network recharge.

Tier 2 (₹50): Google Play redeem codes.

Tier 3 (₹100): Direct cash payouts via UPI.
Beyond content consumption, VidQuest functions as a localized freelance hub. The Micro-Gig Marketplace empowers users to monetize their digital skills by accepting tasks directly from content creators. Opportunities such as custom thumbnail design, short-form video editing, and scriptwriting create a circular, self-sustaining economy within the application
